Walk in prepared

This course prepares you for a class. It is not a class. A form is transmitted body to body, by a teacher who can see you. No web page can do that, and this one will not pretend to. What text can carry is everything else - and there turns out to be a great deal of it.
🧩 The running project: your practice-readiness kit. Each session adds one piece - why you are interested plus a safe balance baseline, your rooting self-tests, a posture checklist, a style shortlist, an evidence-honest expectation - and Session 6 assembles them into the three questions you will ask a prospective teacher and the class you actually choose.
