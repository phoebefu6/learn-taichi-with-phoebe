# learn taichi with phoebe

Everything worth knowing before your first tai chi class - six 45-minute sessions, by Phoebe Fu.

**Live site:** https://phoebefu6.github.io/learn-taichi-with-phoebe/

## What this is - and what it is not

**This course prepares you for a class. It is not a class.** A form is transmitted body to
body, by a teacher who can see you. No web page can do that, and this one does not pretend to.

What text *can* carry turns out to be a great deal: what the practice actually is, where it
really came from, the principles you can understand before you can perform them, what the
research genuinely shows, and how to judge a teacher when no certification means anything.

## The honesty rail

Every substantive claim carries one of three labels:

- **The text says** - a named classical source, dated
- **Tradition holds** - later systematization or school convention, not classical
- **Research says** - a named study with exact numbers and its certainty rating

Unlike most traditional practices, tai chi's "research says" column is often the strong card -
real guideline endorsements and three head-to-head trial wins. Session 5 says so plainly, and
then delivers the limits just as plainly: 7.0% of effect estimates in the entire literature are
high-certainty, and most of tai chi's advantage disappears when the comparator is other exercise
rather than nothing.

## The sessions

1. **What tai chi actually is** - three identities, and the history without the fog machine
2. **Rooting and weight shift** - two facts of balance, with the live lab
3. **松 sōng and the body method** - released, not collapsed; the Ten Essentials
4. **The thirteen postures** - 八门五步, and what push hands is really for
5. **Does it actually work?** - the real numbers, both directions
6. **Finding a teacher** - the five families, and vetting without certification

## Format

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Each session: a 45-minute live arc, inline SVG diagrams, a
3-question quiz, and a pin-able cheat sheet. Session 2 hosts `taichi-live.js`, a rooting lab
that computes real geometry - a convex-hull base of support, a point-in-polygon test, and
recoverable lean as atan(margin / centre-of-mass height). Watch 双重 double-weighting stop being
a scolding and become a measurable trade-off between stability and mobility.

Running project: the learner's **practice-readiness kit**, assembled one piece per session.
