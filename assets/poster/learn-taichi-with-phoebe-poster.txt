STATION 1  WHAT IT ACTUALLY IS
  A martial art, a health practice and a moving meditation, all three at once.
  The legend was asserted in 1867 and quietly retracted in 1881.

STATION 2  ROOTING AND WEIGHT SHIFT
  Two facts about balance, and everything the legs do follows.
  双重 double-weighting is a measurable trade-off, not a scolding.
  The scene shows the base drawn under the feet and a plumb line dropping
  from the centre to the ground.

principles before postures

STATION 3  松 song, RELEASED NOT COLLAPSED
  The principle every teacher names first and every beginner understands last.
  The Ten Essentials, in the order Yang Chengfu gave them.

STATION 4  十三势 THE THIRTEEN POSTURES
  Eight ways of meeting force, five ways of moving. One small closed set.
  The trigram and five-phase overlays are overlays, not doctrine.
  The scene shows push hands: forearms in light contact, nobody pushing hard.

STATION 5  DOES IT ACTUALLY WORK
  Genuine guideline endorsements, and three head-to-head trials.
  The catch is given equal billing, including the hard limits.
  This station's panel is outlined in jade because it is the session that
  cuts against the practice.

then walk into a real room

STATION 6  FINDING A TEACHER
  Five families, and no certification that means anything.
  What the first twelve weeks actually look like.

HOW TO READ THIS WALK
  Numbered stones are the six sessions, in order.
  The jade outline marks the session that cuts against the practice.
  Chinese terms appear only where the course uses them.
